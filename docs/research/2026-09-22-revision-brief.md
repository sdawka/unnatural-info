# Shared revision brief

The user authorized implementing the 50 recommendations from `2026-09-21-book-content-audit.md` with parallel agents and a cohesion pass. Their latest emphasis is that the work should be obviously interesting even when its eventual payoff is not yet apparent.

## Reader and voice

Write an approachable philosophical book for a curious adult without academic training. Preserve wonder, wit, and a personal voice. Lead with an intriguing ordinary situation; let the idea emerge. Avoid course objectives at the start of every passage, sales language, universal diagnoses, and an identical rhetorical template repeated across chapters. No invented true stories. Use imagined examples transparently. No jargon before the experience it names.

## Canonical architecture

- Act I / `volo-ergo-sum`: **What moves us**. Wanting, difference, care, shared knowledge.
- Act II / `initd`: **How we make sense of things**. Boundaries, models, values, categories, costs.
- Act III / `mmm`: **How we learn, create, and change**. Three overlapping modes, not a maturity ladder.
- Learning: Measure, Model, Manipulate. Plain explanation: observe, explain, test. Learning need not deliver control.
- Creating: Map, Move, Make. Plain explanation: find your bearings, try, make.
- Becoming: Marvel, Meander, Manifest. Plain explanation: attend, explore, make room. Manifest means supporting conditions, never wishing outcomes into existence.
- MMM is a family nickname for these three modes. The former single Measure–Model–Manifest sequence is superseded; do not introduce it as the current framework.
- Self/world, map/territory, approach/avoidance are useful distinctions, not established developmental steps. Will is the author's functional lens for orientation; it does not settle consciousness.
- Information is physically carried representation, pattern, or organized practice. A representation's existence does not make its content true.
- The world's existence is distinct from our shared descriptions. Objectivity includes evidence, correction, and whose reports can be heard.
- Good adaptation does not entail moral goodness. Care, consent, fairness, and harm deserve independent reasons.
- A good life can include rest, care, maintenance, limits, and loss. Agreement with the book is not progress.

## Shared narrative thread

The prologue opens on a dinner invitation that has not received a reply. The reader has evidence (silence) and a story (rejection) and a wish (connection). The thread recurs in changed form, not as the opening of every chapter. Act I develops mixed wants and conflicting needs. Act II examines the group chat, labels, seating chart, budget, promises, and what a plan omits. Act III prepares dinner, tests a recipe, adapts to guests, allows surprise, cleans up, and considers who performed invisible work. A shared garden is the larger collective case in chapters 19–20. Other scenes prevent these cooperative cases from standing for all conflict.

## Chapter requirements

Preserve existing filenames, `act`, `order`, chapter numbers, and publication flags. Use `status: draft` honestly. Set English `actTitle` where applicable. Rewrite misleading summaries and overclaiming further-reading notes, and trim lists to sources that do work. Aim roughly 450–800 words of readable prose per numbered chapter, flexible for the argument. Existing beautiful accurate passages can remain. Turn outline assertions into a scene, intelligible explanation, a fair challenge, and a small practice. End with a natural bridge to the next chapter; avoid repeating 'in the next chapter' mechanically. Link verified sources at the claim they inform; use sources from the audit and verify new research on the web. Optional details may use `<details><summary>…</summary>…</details>` with blank lines for Markdown.

Do not add new schema fields unless agreed with parent. Chapter pages will get reading time, draft status, relevant practice links, and optional further reading from shared UI. Exercises link to `/practice/SLUG` using the registry below. All ten will be implemented. Do not depend on the exercise to make the prose intelligible.
