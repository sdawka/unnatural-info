# Information and physical coupling

“Information” in this book means a pattern, representation, or organized practice carried by something physical and able to change what happens next. A map is ink or pixels plus a use. A promise is words, memory, expectations, and actions. A category in a database is code, storage, institutional procedure, and consequences.

This vocabulary distinguishes levels without splitting reality into two substances. The physical world does not depend on our descriptions to exist. A description can still alter the physical world when people use it: a schedule changes who arrives, a diagnosis changes treatment, a law changes access, and a false rumor changes behavior.

The content of a representation can be wrong. A model's physical existence does not make its claims true. Ask what carries the pattern, who interprets it, what practice sustains it, and what evidence could correct it.

Claims about ritual, sacredness, or archetype belong to particular traditions and experiences. The wiki does not turn them into spooky information objects or assert that an unseen spiritual layer causes behavior. Chapter 22 compares named practices in their own terms before offering any structural analogy.
