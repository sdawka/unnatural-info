# Chapter source map

This page maps the current book files to the archive. It contains a preface, prologue, 24 numbered chapters, and an epilogue. The chapter drafts are the authority for claims; wiki pages summarize and question them.

## Framing

- `preface.md` — [Before Meaning](/chapters/preface): motion, pattern, life, and the making of recognizable things.
- `prologue.md` — [On Attempting to Explain Everything (Again)](/chapters/prologue): ordinary maintenance, embodied life, and representations that return to change the world.
- `epilogue.md` — [Use the Blade, Build the World](/chapters/epilogue): distinctions, consequences, ethics, and return.
