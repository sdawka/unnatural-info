# Intellectual lineage

UNNATURAL is a working synthesis, not a school with one founding authority. The sources below do different jobs and often disagree.

## Act I — What moves us

- **Damasio:** somatic signals can contribute to decision-making; this does not settle consciousness or reduce thought to feeling.
- **Spinoza and Schopenhauer:** philosophical vocabularies for striving and will, treated as proposals rather than biological evidence.
- **Varela, Thompson, and Rosch:** cognition as embodied and enacted in organism–environment activity.
- **Buber, Murdoch, Frankfurt, and Wolf:** care, attention, endorsement, and meaningful engagement complicate a simple theory of desire.
- **Nagel and Fricker:** objectivity is an achievement from situated perspectives, and shared knowledge can exclude testimony.

## Act II — How we make sense of things

- **Maturana and Varela:** living boundaries and organization.
- **Gibson and embodied approaches:** perception can be action-guiding without requiring one exhaustive inner map.
- **Korzybski, Peirce, Popper, and Kuhn:** maps, signs, inquiry, error, and changing models.
- **Searle, Bowker and Star, Hacking, and Scott:** institutions and classifications allocate consequences.
- **Shannon and Floridi:** information as a technical and philosophical vocabulary, not a spooky extra substance.

## Act III — How we learn, create, and change

- **Dewey, Schön, Kolb, and Argyris:** inquiry, making, reflection, and revision.
- **Ashby and cybernetics:** feedback and limits on regulation; not a proof that all life shares one loop.
- **Roediger and Karpicke:** retrieval as one concrete learning practice.
- **Gollwitzer and Sheeran:** implementation intentions as a bounded planning tool.
- **Hadot and named contemplative traditions:** repeated practices that should be described in their own terms before comparison.

## How to read this list

A source can support an example, provide an objection, or offer a vocabulary. Its presence does not validate every claim in the book. Where MMM resembles another three-part account, the resemblance is an analogy to examine, not independent corroboration.
