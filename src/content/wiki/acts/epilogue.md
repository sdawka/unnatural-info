# Epilogue — Use the blade, build the world

The epilogue returns to the opening situation and asks what the reader will do with a framework that remains partial. Distinctions are useful because they make action possible; they are dangerous when treated as essences. Maps need correction. Practices need consequences. Intentions do not cancel harm.

The ethical conclusion is deliberately wider than keeping a process efficient. Ask about consent, fairness, preventable harm, access, maintenance, and whose future choices remain open. Rest, care, outside help, repair, and accepting loss are legitimate responses alongside changing a model or trying a new action.
