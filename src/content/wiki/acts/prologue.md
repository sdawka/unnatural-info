# Prologue — A method for looking again

> An ordinary reach opens onto a larger question: how natural beings make pictures of a world, then use them to change it.

The prologue introduces the book's question and method together. Thought belongs to living bodies, yet shared descriptions become knowledge, technologies, institutions, and obligations. A recipe changes a meal; a design helps make a bridge; a rule shapes lives beyond its author's lifetime. The inquiry runs from ordinary experience into the worlds people build together.

Start with something observable; name the interpretation; keep uncertainty visible; use research where it can answer the question; and treat the framework as revisable. A reader's experience is evidence about how a situation appears, not proof of a universal biological history.

The book's three acts are **What moves us**, **How we make sense of things**, and **How we learn, create, and change**. The archive retains the older titles Volo Ergo Sum, `init.d`, and MMM as secondary names.
