# Prologue — A method for looking again

> A missed reply separates event, story, wish, and unknown.

The prologue is the book's method. Start with something observable; name the interpretation; keep uncertainty visible; use research where it can answer the question; and treat the framework as revisable. A reader's experience is evidence about how a situation appears, not proof of a universal biological history.

The book's three acts are **What moves us**, **How we make sense of things**, and **How we learn, create, and change**. The archive retains the older titles Volo Ergo Sum, `init.d`, and MMM as secondary names.
