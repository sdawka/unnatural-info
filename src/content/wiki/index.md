# UNNATURAL Wiki — Index

The wiki is the book's working archive: concepts, sources, themes, and open questions. The readable route is **What moves us → How we make sense of things → How we learn, create, and change**. The archive keeps deeper context for readers who want to follow a name, dispute, or source.

## Concepts

| Page | Summary |
|------|---------|
| [Volo Ergo Sum](concepts/volo-ergo-sum.md) | A proposed lens: living systems are organized around conditions they approach, avoid, preserve, or change. |
| [The Three Blades](concepts/the-three-blades.md) | Three useful distinctions: self/world, world/model, and good/bad. They are lenses, not proven developmental stages. |
| [Thingification](concepts/thingification.md) | How names and categories make continuous situations easier to handle, and how they can harden into false essences. |
| [The MMM Loop](concepts/the-mmm-loop.md) | The book's family name for three overlapping modes of learning, creating, and becoming; a proposed synthesis rather than a universal mechanism. |
| [Foundational Dualities](concepts/foundational-dualities.md) | A study of boundaries and oppositions, including gradients, exceptions, and the prior choices that make a category useful. |
| [Information / Physical Coupling](concepts/information-physical-duality.md) | Representations and organized practices are physically carried and can have physical consequences; their content can still be false. |
| [Layered Realism](concepts/layered-realism.md) | A working way to distinguish physical objects, artifacts, social facts, abstractions, and experiences without treating them as separate substances. |
| [The Denial of Death (Becker)](concepts/denial-of-death.md) | Becker's influential interpretation of mortality and culture, presented as one contested account among several. |
