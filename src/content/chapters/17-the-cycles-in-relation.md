At ten minutes past seven, three people are doing competent but incompatible things. One keeps reading recipes. One has begun chopping without checking what anyone else planned. One is gazing into the open refrigerator, hoping dinner will reveal itself.

No mode is bad. Each can be wrong for the moment.

Learning helps when a consequential uncertainty can be reduced. Why did the sauce split? What does the guest actually avoid? It becomes less useful when more information will not change the decision, or when research is protecting us from making one.

Creating helps when contact with reality would teach more than another plan. Cook a small batch. Move the chairs. Send the first honest sentence. It becomes less useful when action is scattering effort, imposing on someone else, or disguising the absence of a purpose.

Becoming helps when force and premature categories are closing attention. Sit with the music. Let several possibilities remain alive. Notice the person rather than assigning them a role in your solution. It becomes less useful when openness becomes a noble name for postponement.

These are situational judgments, not stages of development. Stress does not send everyone down a universal ladder, and age does not confer receptivity. A child may notice what every adult missed. An expert may need deliberate measurement. Any person may need two modes at once: make an apology while remaining open to a response they cannot control.

A useful question is: **what kind of response could this situation support?**

- Understand more when relevant evidence is available and the stakes justify looking.
- Try something small when feedback would be informative and the attempt is safe enough.
- Make room when the outcome depends on other agents, living processes, or meanings that cannot be forced.
- Repair a relationship when harm or broken trust is the central fact.
- Change the conditions or seek help when the obstacle is material, institutional, medical, or beyond one person's capacity.
- Rest when effort itself has become part of the damage.
- Live with a loss when no mode can restore what is gone.

The last four prevent MMM from swallowing the world. A person unable to afford rent does not chiefly need a richer inner model of money. Someone in an unsafe workplace may need allies, protection, or an exit. Grief is not stalled Creating. Rest need not be a clever technique for producing more later.

How do you know when to switch? Look for evidence, not a personality label. If new observations keep changing the explanation, continue Learning. If the explanation has stabilized but nothing has met the world, try Creating. If repeated effort is narrowing perception or violating another person's agency, pause and receive. If exhaustion, danger, or isolation dominates, the next move may be support.

Sometimes the goal deserves challenge before any mode is chosen. Becoming faster at answering every message may intensify an unreasonable expectation. Improving the dinner may matter less than noticing who has cooked and cleaned every week.

The modes are instruments. Judgment includes knowing when to put them down. The next chapter follows what happens when we use them badly, lose what we learned, and begin again without pretending every return is upward.
